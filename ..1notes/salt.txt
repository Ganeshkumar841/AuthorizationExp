In cryptographhy and password security, salt and salt rounds refer to two key concepts:

what is salt:
salt is a unique,randomly genrated string of bits added to password before it gets passed thorugh a hashing function.

without salt -> Hash = hash("secret123")
with salt    -> Hash = hash("secret123"+"r8$kL9@z")

why salt necessary ?
-> prevents the Rainbow table attacks
   hackers precompute the list of containing billons of common passwords alongside thier resulting values
-> prevents identical hashes for identical passwords
   if two users happen to choose same password as thier password,plain hashing makes thier database eniterly look identical.with salt they will look diff.

why saltRounds ?
-> In bcrypt.hash(password,10) -> 10 specifies saltrounds(cost factor)
-> Salt rounds determine how many iterations (2^cost) the hashing algo runs.
->a cost factor of 10 means bcrypt processes the hashing loop 2^10 times
